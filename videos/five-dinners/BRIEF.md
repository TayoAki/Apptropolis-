# Brief: Five Dinners — "Five dinners. One list. Leftovers first."

- **Format:** Product Film (no footage). Recreated UI, in code.
- **Story:** the weekly habit in one pass: your household's five dinners, priced; one shopping
  list in whole packs, ticked off in the store; dinners cooked through the week, with what's left
  of each pack tracked; next week planned around the leftovers.
- **Audience:** cost-conscious US households that shop weekly (the app prices at Aldi US).
- **Destinations:** Reels / Shorts / TikTok (vertical master) plus a 16:9 cut.
- **Runtime:** ~21 s. **Masters:** 1080 × 1920 @ 30 fps; 1920 × 1080 @ 30 fps, recomposed.
- **Sound:** silent (no music bed supplied). **Captions:** none; the type band carries the story.
- **Product evidence:** `five-dinners/` at this branch, its App Store captures in
  `five-dinners/store/screenshots/captures/` (retaken with the recipe images), and the source for
  labels and numbers. The demo household (4 people, $65 budget, no pork) is the one the screenshot
  script builds through the real app.
- **Brand:** Larder theme from `five-dinners/theme/tokens.ts`; the app uses one sans typeface.
- **Truth boundary:**
  - may show: real screens with the demo household's real numbers; recipe images, which are
    AI-generated illustrations (the app says so in Settings and on the recipe page).
  - must not show or claim: savings figures, "save money", "healthy", "AI", competitor names, a
    price for the app, anything paid unlabelled. Week 2 (planning around leftovers) is a paid
    feature: label it.
  - naming: the app's own screens show "Aldi · ZIP 60601" as its price source; keep it as the app
    shows it, and flag the trademark question to the owner.
- **CTA:** "Coming soon on iPhone" (not on the App Store yet).
