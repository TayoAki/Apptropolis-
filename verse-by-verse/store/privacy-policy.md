# Verse by Verse Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

This policy explains what Verse by Verse ("the app") does with your information. "We" means the
app's developer.

## What the app collects
Nothing. The app has no account and does not send your information to us or to anyone else.

- **What you save** (your reading place, saved verses, folders and settings) is stored only on
  your phone.
- **Reminders** you turn on are scheduled by your phone. We don't receive them.
- The app has **no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking**, and it does not
  use your location, camera, photos or contacts.

## Links you open
The Privacy policy and Terms of use links open in your browser, and sharing a verse hands it to the
app you choose. Those services have their own privacy policies.

## Deleting your information
Delete the app to remove everything it stored on your phone.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

## Changes
If the app's features change, this policy changes with them and the date above shows when.
