# Verse by Verse Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

This policy explains what Verse by Verse ("the app") collects, why, and the choices you have. "We"
means the app's developer.

## What the app collects
- **What you save** (your reading place, saved verses, folders and settings) stays on your phone.
  We don't receive it.
- **Reminders** you set are scheduled by your phone. We don't receive them.
- **Error reports:** when the app runs into a problem, it sends the error message, the screen it
  happened on and the kind of phone, with a random install ID, so the problem can be fixed. Reports
  never include your name, email or what you saved.

The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not use your
location, camera, photos or contacts.

## Who it's shared with
Error reports are processed by App Builder, the platform the app is built on, on our behalf (its
servers run on Railway in the United States). We may also disclose information when the law
requires it. We don't sell your information.

## How long it's kept
What you save: on your phone until you delete it or the app. Error reports: 90 days after the error
was last seen.

## Your choices
Turn off reminders in the app or in your phone's settings. To ask about or delete error reports
connected to your install, contact us below. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or
California) you may have further rights, and you can complain to your data protection authority.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes
If the app's features change, this policy changes with them and the date above shows when.
